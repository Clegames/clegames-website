GDPR Privacy Policy

Clegames, Inc.  ·  European Data Protection

Clegames, Inc., (collectively referred to as "CLEGAMES", "we", "our" or "us") provide this privacy policy to explain how we collect, use, and share information. For the purposes of European data protection laws, CLEGAMES is the data controller of your personal information.

This Policy covers CLEGAMES's handling of information we receive about end users of third-party mobile applications ("Apps") that use our Software Development Kit ("SDK"), Application Programming Interface ("API"), Demand Side Platform ("DSP") or other technologies (collectively, the "CLEGAMES Services"), and information we receive about Users while Users interact with platforms, applications, websites or other services provided by CLEGAMES's business partners.

This Policy is subject to any service agreement between CLEGAMES and you. By using the CLEGAMES Service, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. We evaluate this Policy and procedures to implement improvements from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you by means of a prominent notice on CLEGAMES Site prior to the change becoming effective.

1. Introduction

This Policy applies to information collection and use by CLEGAMES while Users: (i) playing the game by the CLEGAMES Services; (ii) interact with the CLEGAMES Services; or (iii) interact with platforms, applications, websites or other services provided by CLEGAMES's business partners.

Our technology enables our partners to show Users more relevant advertising that is based on Users' interests and behaviors. The CLEGAMES Services may link to third party websites, Apps or other third party content. Please note that we cannot control the treatment of a User's information by such third parties.

2. How We Collect Users' Information

Depending on the nature of a User's interaction with the CLEGAMES Services, or any service provided by CLEGAMES's business partners, we may collect information directly from such User or from our business partners (e.g. mobile application developers, Ad Exchange platforms, and advertisers).

For example, when we partner with an Ad Exchange platform and the Ad Exchange platform initiates an ad request to our DSP, it may share User's information with us. Where required, the publisher will obtain User consent for and on our behalf for the processing of User personal information to display advertising on a User's device. A User can withdraw their consent at any time.

3. Information We May Collect From Users

When you view ads served by CLEGAMES Service or interact with services provided by CLEGAMES's business partners, we and/or our business partners may collect your information including the following:

  • Device make, device model, operating system, device type and properties
  • Carrier, name and properties of mobile application
  • Country, time zone and locale settings (country and preferred language)
  • Network connection type, IP address, Internet browser user-agent
  • Identifier for advertisers (IDFA), Google Advertising ID (AAID / GAID)
  • SDK version, timestamp, API key, application version, OS version
  • Network status such as WiFi, geo-location of your device (GPS or other data)
  • International mobile equipment identity (IMEI)
  • Content type and ad type (text, image, or video based ad)
  • Device event information such as crashes, system activity, hardware settings
  • Browser type, browser language, date and time of your request and referral URL

4. How We Use Users' Personal Information

We use your personal information to display advertising on your device, which may include interest-based advertising customized to individuals' inferred interests, preferences, locations and other features. Where required, the publisher will obtain User consent on our behalf. A User can withdraw their consent at any time.

Where necessary in our legitimate interests we use your personal information: (i) to understand and analyze your usage trends and preferences and improve our CLEGAMES Service; or (ii) for purpose of anti-fraud and traffic quality monitoring in order to perform the contractual obligations to our business partners.

We may also use personal information about Users to comply with applicable laws, regulations or codes of practice, and to protect our legitimate interest and protect the rights or property of us or any other third parties.

5. Disclosure of Users' Personal Information

We may disclose the User's personal information to our business partners, including our advertisers, to report the measurement of Users' interactions with the Apps, and for anti-fraud and traffic quality monitoring in order to perform the contractual obligations to our business partners. These business partners may use the Users' personal information to show Users relevant ads, measure Users' ad/site interaction, and better understand site and app traffic usage.

We may also share aggregate or anonymous information with other third parties in accordance with the terms of this Policy.

We may share Users' personal information as required or permitted by law to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process or government request, or when we believe in good faith that disclosure is legally required or otherwise necessary to protect our rights and property, or the rights, property or safety of others.

6. International Transfers

When you are located in the EEA, personal information collected from you, including through our CLEGAMES Service, will be transferred to recipients in countries located outside the EEA (including in China, US, India, Korea, Singapore, Australia, Japan, Canada, France, Ireland, London, Germany, Brazil), which do not provide a similar or adequate level of protection to that provided by countries in the EEA.

Where required by law, the publisher and/or advertiser will obtain your consent to the transfer of your personal information to recipients located outside the EEA. You may withdraw your consent at any time.

7. Your Data Rights

If you are in the EEA, you have a right to (subject to a limited number of exceptions):

  • Request access to and rectification or erasure of your personal information
  • Obtain restriction of processing or to object to processing of your personal information
  • Ask for a copy of your personal information to be provided in a digital format
  • Lodge a complaint about the processing of your personal information with your local data protection authority in the EEA

To exercise this right please contact help@clegames.com

8. Security of Users' Personal Information

The security of User's information is our utmost priority, and we take strict measures in order to protect our data from unauthorized access, use, disclosure or destruction of data. We have implemented physical, technical and administrative security measures for the Services that comply with applicable laws and industry standards.

For example, we use firewalls, encryption technology and other automated software designed to protect against fraud and identity theft. Our data is only stored in centers that provide high-level security for User's information. Physical access is strictly controlled both at the perimeter and at building ingress points by our staff utilizing video surveillance and other electronic means.

CLEGAMES may retain the information relating to you collected from our business partners as necessary for the provision of the services, internal analytical purposes, or to comply with its legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce agreements.

9. Children

We ensure that we comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and does not knowingly collect personal information about Users of Apps directed to children under the age of thirteen (13). For more detailed information please visit our COPPA policy.

10. Marketing

We may contact you periodically by e-mail to provide information regarding our products, services and content that may be of interest to you. If applicable law requires that we receive your consent before we send you certain types of marketing communications, we will only send you those types of communications after receiving your consent.

If you decide at any time that you no longer wish to receive such communications from us, you may follow the unsubscribe instructions provided in any of the communications or request to be opted-out via email to help@clegames.com.

If a parent or guardian becomes aware that his or her child has provided us with information without their consent, he or she should promptly contact us and we will take reasonable steps to ensure that such information is deleted from our files.

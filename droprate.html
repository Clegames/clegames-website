General Rules

Equipment level appearance range
  • Dragon Orb Lv 1: Spawns at Han Bikwang's level ±1 (33.33% each)
  • Dragon Orb Lv 2+: Spawns at Han Bikwang's level ±2 (20% each)
Fixed effect values are determined uniformly at random between min and max in 0.01 increments, then rounded:
  • ATK / DEF / HP — displayed to 1 decimal place
  • Attack Speed — displayed to 2 decimal places
Attack speed on weapons is determined by the Dragon Orb level at the time the item is obtained.
Random effects appear on Rare tier and above.
  • Rare ~ Epic: 1 random effect
  • Legendary ~ Immortal: 2 random effects
Random effect values do not scale with equipment level.

Random Effect Ranges

Each random effect has an equal 12.5% chance of appearing. All 8 effects can roll on any tier. Values are uniform random between min and max (0.01 increments), then rounded. No scaling with equipment level.

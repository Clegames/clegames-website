This CCPA Privacy Policy applies to residents of California and supplements our general Privacy Policy. It is provided pursuant to the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018 ("CCPA") and the California Privacy Rights Act ("CPRA"), and describes how Clegames, Inc. ("Company", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and discloses personal information of California consumers.

Any terms defined in the CCPA have the same meaning when used in this notice. This policy covers the 12-month period preceding its effective date.

2. Purposes of Collection

We collect personal information for the following business purposes:

  • Providing, maintaining, and improving our games and services
  • Processing in-app purchases and verifying transactions
  • Serving interest-based advertising through third-party ad networks
  • Fraud detection, security, and abuse prevention
  • Analytics and performance measurement
  • Customer support and responding to inquiries
  • Complying with legal obligations

3. Sharing of Personal Information

In the preceding 12 months, we have disclosed personal information to the following categories of third parties for business purposes:

  • Advertising partners — to serve personalized ads (e.g. AdMob, Meta Audience Network, AppLovin)
  • Analytics providers — to understand usage and improve services (e.g. Firebase, AppsFlyer)
  • Payment processors — to complete in-app purchases (via App Store / Google Play)
  • Cloud infrastructure — to host and operate our services
  • Law enforcement / legal authorities — when required by law

We may share identifiers and internet activity information with advertising partners, which may constitute a "sale" or "sharing" under the CCPA. See the Do Not Sell or Share section below to opt out.

4. Your California Privacy Rights

As a California resident, you have the following rights under the CCPA/CPRA:

  • Right to Know — Request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you
  • Right to Delete — Request deletion of personal information we have collected, subject to certain exceptions
  • Right to Correct — Request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out — Opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information
  • Right to Limit Use of Sensitive PI — Limit our use of sensitive personal information
  • Right to Non-Discrimination — We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights

To submit a request, contact us at help@clegames.com with the subject line "CCPA Request". We will respond within 45 days.

⛔ Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

Under the CCPA, California residents have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of their personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising purposes.

Clegames may share device identifiers and usage data with third-party advertising networks (such as AdMob, Meta, AppLovin) to serve personalized ads. This may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information under the CCPA.

To opt out, you can:

  • Use your device's Limit Ad Tracking (iOS) or Opt out of Ads Personalization (Android) setting
  • Reset your Advertising ID in your device settings
  • Submit a direct opt-out request to us by email

5. Retention of Personal Information

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law. The criteria we use to determine retention periods include:

  • How long the data is needed to provide services and operate our business
  • Whether we are subject to a legal or contractual obligation to retain the data
  • Whether retention is necessary for fraud prevention or security purposes

6. Children Under 13

We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16 years of age. If a consumer is under 13 years of age, we require affirmative authorization from the consumer's parent or guardian before selling or sharing their personal information. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13 without parental consent, please contact us immediately at help@clegames.com.

7. Authorized Agents

You may designate an authorized agent to make a CCPA request on your behalf. To do so, please provide written authorization signed by you and submit it along with the request. We may require direct verification of your identity before processing the request.
